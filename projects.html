The problem

Operators were spending hours manually annotating 2D plans, with no visibility into progress, no secure data handling, and no centralized dashboard. Reviews and approvals happened over scattered emails — slowing operational throughput.

The solution

A React + Node.js platform with TensorFlow.js-assisted annotation, Annotorious for plan interaction, JWT + AES authentication, and an AWS-hosted admin dashboard with real-time notifications. Outcome: 25% operational efficiency gain and a secure, scalable pipeline.
